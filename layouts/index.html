About Me

I am a Graduate Researcher in the Department of Mathematics at NC State University, where I am fortunate to work under the supervision of Dr. Chao Chen. My academic and professional affiliations include:

  • M.S. in Applied Mathematics, North Carolina State University (2024)
  • M.Sc. in Mathematics, National Institute of Technology, Tiruchirappalli (2022)
  • Research Intern, NIT Tiruchirappalli (2021)
  • B.Sc. in Mathematics, Physics, and Chemistry, Ch. Charan Singh University, Meerut (2019)

Research Interests

My research focuses on numerical linear algebra, with an emphasis on scalable, matrix-free algorithms for large-scale scientific computing. I develop randomized and hierarchical techniques for structured operators, particularly in regimes where global low-rank approaches are inefficient.
