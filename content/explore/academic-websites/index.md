I maintain these open-source templates for professional academic websites, from a lightweight Hugo starter to a feature-rich Jekyll design:

### 1. Academic Starter

A lightweight **Hugo** starter with a simple structure and minimal maintenance, covering the essential components of a professional academic profile.

[See Live Demo](https://bhattharshit.github.io/academic-starter/) [Repository](https://github.com/BhattHarshit/academic-starter/)

### 2. Academic Builder

A flexible **Hugo** template designed for easy setup and customization, with a richer set of built-in features.

[See Live Demo](https://bhattharshit.github.io/academic-builder/) [Repository](https://github.com/BhattHarshit/academic-builder/)

### 3. Academic Signature

A feature-rich **Jekyll** academic website based on **al-folio**, with a redesigned profile-focused interface and extensive support for academic content.

[See Live Demo](https://bhattharshit.github.io/academic-signature/) [Repository](https://github.com/BhattHarshit/academic-signature)
