I am excited to announce the public release of **MathNews** — a professional, elegant, and searchable portal that brings together **Events**, **Jobs**, **Mathematical News**, **Calls & Contributions**, and **more** across the global mathematics community.

### Why MathNews?
Today, information is fragmented across many institute websites, email lists, newsletters, social media, etc. Access to the information is based on the hierarchy, and excellent opportunities either do not reach people in need or get lost in the noise, making researchers spend time tracking updates. **MathNews** centralizes essential mathematical tools and academic updates, making them searchable while presenting them with the clarity and polish the field deserves.

### What’s live today
A preview of the upcoming activities is available on **MathNews**, which shows how we will provide easy access to the numerous **Events**, **Jobs**, and **Calls** in one place — with easily discoverable formatting, necessary Mathematical tools, News across the field, and many more. 

### Coming next (Roadmap)
- **Automated Ingestion**: Scheduled fetchers for institute sites, RSS feeds, and trusted sources.
- **Sophisticated Tools**: Data-informed pages to navigate journals, metrics, rankings, and more.
- **Newsletter**: A low-noise brief info of the most relevant items you select.

### Who is it for?
- **Students** hunting for schools, programs, internships, jobs, and research opportunities.
- **Researchers & Faculty** looking for workshops, conferences, and calls.
- **Departments & Societies** that want a wider, credible channel to share updates.
