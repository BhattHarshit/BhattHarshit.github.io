I am delighted to share that I have joined the **Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics (SIAM) Student Chapter at NC State University** as **President**.

The SIAM Student Chapter brings together students and faculty who share a passion for applied and computational mathematics. It serves as a platform to exchange ideas, promote research collaboration, and build connections across disciplines.

As President, I look forward to organizing engaging seminars, outreach initiatives, and professional development events that strengthen our community and highlight the impact of applied mathematics in science and engineering.
