I am honored to have been selected for the **Graduate Student Association (GSA) Outstanding Teaching Assistant Award** at NC State University on **April 22, 2026**.

Presented by the [NC State Graduate Student Association](https://gsa.ncsu.edu/), this distinction acknowledges a small number of graduate students across the university for sustained commitment to teaching, mentorship, and student success, following a competitive nomination and review process.

Teaching has been a meaningful part of my graduate experience—shaping how I communicate ideas, engage with students, and think about learning more broadly. I am grateful to the students, faculty, and colleagues who have supported and influenced this journey.

I also recognize the many graduate students who contribute deeply to teaching every day, and I share this recognition with them.
