I am happy to share that I have joined the Asian Students Association (ASA) at NC State as an Advisor!

ASA is a dynamic student-led organization dedicated to promoting cultural awareness, fostering social connections, and supporting the personal development of students across the Asian diaspora.

I look forward to supporting ASA's mission by providing guidance, mentorship, and institutional insight. It is a privilege to contribute to a community that celebrates cultural diversity and fosters meaningful connections.

For more information about ASA’s events and initiatives, visit [linktr.ee/ncsuasa](https://linktr.ee/ncsuasa).
