philosophy: |
  Research is much like climbing a mountain — we plan the trip, search for grip, climb a little, stumble, and slip. Sometimes, we lose sight, hold things tight, change the route, and it’s time to reboot. We crawl a little, rise again, each fall a lesson; nothing is vain. We look behind, feel amazed, then look ahead — so much still to chase. We pause momentarily, catching our breath, and climb again, defying the depth.

My work primarily focuses on numerical linear algebra, inverse problems, and PDE-constrained optimization. I am enthusiastic about developing efficient solvers for large-scale systems and leveraging data-driven methodologies in computational science.
