## **Course Description**  
MA 513 introduces the central ideas and techniques of complex analysis. The course develops both the algebraic and geometric viewpoints of complex numbers, then studies analytic functions, contour integration, power series, residues, and conformal mappings. This is a well-established online and asynchronous course where the detailed lectures are recorded by [Prof. Tye Lidman](https://math.sciences.ncsu.edu/people/tlidman/).

**Topics Covered**
- Complex numbers and geometry of the complex plane  
- Limits, continuity, complex differentiation, and the Cauchy–Riemann equations  
- Elementary functions, complex logarithms, branches, and multivalued functions  
- Contours, complex integration, and the Cauchy integral theorems  
- Taylor and Laurent series  
- Zeros, singularities, poles, and residues  
- Evaluation of real integrals using contour integration  
- The Fundamental Theorem of Algebra, Liouville’s theorem, and related results  
- Conformal mappings and harmonic functions

## Learning Outcomes
By the end of the course, students will be able to:
- Work with complex numbers from algebraic and geometric perspectives  
- Determine analyticity using limits, the Cauchy–Riemann equations, power series, and contour integrals  
- Compute and estimate contour integrals  
- Work correctly with branches of multivalued functions such as the complex logarithm  
- Classify singularities and apply the residue theorem  
- Use complex-variable methods to evaluate real integrals and study harmonic functions  
- Analyze complex functions geometrically through conformal mappings  
- Write clear proofs and carry out accurate computations in complex analysis

## Tips for Self-Guided Learning
- Keep a consistent weekly schedule. In an asynchronous course, falling behind can make later topics much harder
- Draw the complex plane, contours, branch cuts, and mapped regions whenever possible 
- Learn each major theorem together with its hypotheses, geometric meaning, and a typical application
- Attempt problems before consulting solutions, then rewrite incomplete arguments clearly
